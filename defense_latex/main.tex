\chapter{Hybrid Split Parallelism}

\paraq{What has been established before this chapter, and what problem remains?}
In the preceding chapters we built \textbf{gsplit} (Spara), a partition-aware distributed
GNN training system, and showed that it trains correctly and outperforms the state of the art
across a range of settings. In gsplit, each GPU owns a METIS partition weighted by sampling
frequency, and sampling, feature loading, and the model forward/backward exchange only
boundary data across GPUs via NCCL. This chapter starts from a tension our ablation studies exposed:
\emph{the gain from gsplit is not uniform}---depending on the setting, gsplit can be faster or slower
than a data-parallel baseline.

\paraq{Which settings make gsplit's gain inconsistent, and why?}
Two model parameters change the cost of the cross-partition \textbf{shuffles}:
\textbf{depth} (every additional layer adds another all-to-all shuffle) and
\textbf{hidden size} (every shuffle moves a larger feature volume). Both also change the computation
and data-loading savings that splitting provides, so neither has a consistent effect: as models grow
deeper or wider, gsplit's training time relative to the baseline improves in some settings and
degrades in others, depending on the GNN model, the graph, and the interconnect. 

\paraq{What does this chapter do about it, and in what order?}
This chapter (i)~\emph{quantifies} how gsplit's gain over the DGL baseline varies
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}); (ii)~proposes \emph{two hybrid parallelism
schemes}---a data-parallel hybrid and a pipeline hybrid---that address the communication costs exposed by our system
(Section~\ref{sec:hybrids}); (iii)~introduces a \emph{communication-centric roofline} on which
every layer of every strategy is one point, so that the bottleneck of each layer can be read off
(Section~\ref{sec:roofline}); (iv)~draws that roofline for representative settings of the sweep and
looks for the patterns a configuration algorithm can use (Section~\ref{sec:predicted}); and
(v)~checks how close the roofline's picture comes to the measured winners (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
