\chapter{Hybrid Split Parallelism}

\paraq{What has been established before this chapter, and what problem remains?}
In the preceding chapters we built \textbf{gsplit} (Spara), a partition-aware distributed
GNN training system, and showed that it trains correctly and performs decently across a
range of settings: each GPU owns a METIS partition (weighted by sampling frequency), and
sampling, feature loading, and the model forward/backward exchange only boundary data
across GPUs via NCCL. This chapter starts from a tension our ablation studies exposed:
\emph{the gain from gsplit is not uniform}---in some settings it is eroded or lost entirely.

\paraq{Which settings erode gsplit's advantage, and why?}
Two settings drive the cross-partition \textbf{shuffle} cost up until it dominates:
\textbf{deeper models} (every additional layer adds another all-to-all shuffle), and
\textbf{larger hidden sizes} (every shuffle moves a larger feature volume).
In these settings potential savings in data loading and computation are lost due to the increasing cost of splitting. 
%  In these
% regimes the communication gsplit was designed to minimize instead becomes its bottleneck,
% and a simpler baseline can win.

\paraq{What does this chapter do about it, and in what order?}
This chapter (i)~\emph{quantifies} where gsplit underperforms against the DGL and Quiver
baselines (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}); (ii)~proposes \emph{two hybrid parallelism
schemes}---a data-parallel hybrid and a pipeline hybrid---that should relieve those
settings (Section~\ref{sec:hybrids}); (iii)~builds an approximate \emph{cost model} that
gives the per-layer choice between pulling and recomputing a mathematical basis, aiming for a
reasonably close choice rather than exact epoch times (Section~\ref{sec:roofline});
(iv)~uses that model on the problematic settings to \emph{predict the best Quiver/Split
configuration} for each (Section~\ref{sec:predicted}); and (v)~reports the \emph{final
runtime results} of the hybrids at those configurations (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
